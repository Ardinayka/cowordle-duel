How to play

Inter font licence

English word-list credits

Teal ●: correct position. Amber ↔: wrong position. Slate ×: absent or no extra copy.

English accepts a broad UK/US word list, including plurals and inflections. Secret answers are curated separately. This release supports English only.

Friend rooms expire after 24 hours. Refreshing preserves the match and clock on the same browser tab. Leaving forfeits a competitive match; leaving Co-op ends the team game.

A small friends beta

Play as a guest or sign in with Google to save your username, friends and avatar. Rooms last up to 24 hours. Friend requests expire after seven days and invitations after five minutes.

Connection status helps you see when an opponent is reconnecting. It does not change the match timer. You can block signed-in players from their avatar or Friends.

There are no ads or analytics scripts in this beta. Read the full privacy notice for data use, retention and your rights, or send a privacy request.

Delete your account?

This removes your profile, friends and challenges, and signs you out on all devices. You can keep playing as a guest.

Leave this match?

Leaving ends your participation. Competitive matches count as a forfeit.
