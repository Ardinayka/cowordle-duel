How to play

Inter font licence

English word-list credits

Teal ●: correct position. Amber ↔: wrong position. Slate ×: absent or no extra copy.

English accepts a broad UK/US word list, including plurals and inflections. Secret answers are curated separately. PT-PT is still a preview; write accents and Ç as plain letters.

Friend rooms expire after 24 hours. Refreshing preserves the match and clock on the same browser tab. Leaving forfeits a competitive match; leaving Co-op ends the team game.

A small friends beta

Guest play needs only a nickname. This browser remembers your language and match settings. A tab session keeps your room seat; closing the tab can lose access to that seat.

Friend rooms hold nicknames, guesses and match times. Rooms become inaccessible after 24 hours; expired records are removed when new rooms are created. Anyone with a room link and site access can take the available seat. Share it privately.

Optional Google sign-in stores your Google account identifier and display name. We do not request contacts, Gmail or Drive access. Sign-in lasts up to seven days. Delete account removes this profile and its sign-in sessions; it does not erase nicknames in existing rooms. Practice results stay in this visit.

Online search temporarily stores your nickname and a random browser identifier to find a player. Cancel stops the search; inactive searches expire.

There are no ads or analytics scripts in this beta. Hosting and Google may process technical data for their services. Lexivanto is a working name; this is an independent game, with no affiliation to other word-game brands.

Delete your account?

This removes your saved profile and signs you out on all devices. You can keep playing as a guest.

Leave this match?

Leaving ends your participation. Competitive matches count as a forfeit.
