A co-op word game for two

Work out the hidden five-letter word together. You take turns on one shared board and win or lose as a team.

Play now

How Co-op works

  1. On Play, choose Play a friend or Practice vs bot, then choose Co-op.
  2. Take turns: one guess each, on the same board. Every clue helps both of you.
  3. Solve the word within your shared attempts to win together.

With a friend, the host chooses six, ten or unlimited shared attempts and can add a turn timer; by default there is no clock.

Read the clues together

Each letter gets a clue shape: a square with a tick for the right letter in the right place, a circle for a letter that is in the word but somewhere else, and a flat, dim tile for a letter that is not in the word. The shapes do not rely on color. More on clues.

Questions

Can I play Co-op on my own?
Not yet. Co-op needs two players, so send a friend an invitation link. To practice on your own, play Duel or Race against the bot.
Does my friend need to sign in?
No. Send an invitation link and they can join as a guest.
Is Co-op rated?
No. Co-op is never rated.
