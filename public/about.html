About Lexivanto

Five letters. Three ways to play together.

Lexivanto is an independent English word game for your browser, created by bully. Play Duel, Race or Co-op with a friend, find an online opponent, or practise with a bot.

Built around playing together

The idea is simple: turn a five-letter puzzle into something you can share. Compare clues in Duel, chase the first correct answer in Race, or take turns working it out in Co-op.

Lexivanto is shaped by playtesting and player feedback. If an invitation is confusing, a word seems missing or something gets in the way of playing, tell us what happened.

A growing friends beta

No download is needed. Choose a mode and play as a guest, or sign in with Google to choose a unique username, save friends and use your avatar across devices.

There are no rankings, paid advantages, advertisements or analytics scripts in this release. Practice results last for the current visit. Your feedback helps guide the next improvements.

Questions or feedback?

Send a message about a bug, an idea or a privacy question. Read how your data is handled.

Lexivanto is not affiliated with other word-game brands.
