About Lexivanto

A browser word game for friendly competition and cooperation.

Lexivanto brings together Duel, Race and Co-op. Challenge a friend, find an online opponent or practise with an adjustable bot. You can play as a guest without installing an app.

A growing beta

The game is in beta. English and a Portuguese preview are available. This release focuses on clear clues, simple invitations and three core modes.

Your account

Google sign-in is optional. It supplies a saved display name, not cloud match history. Practice results last for this visit. You can sign out or delete your profile through the game's Profile menu.

For the current data-handling details, open Privacy & beta notes in the game menu. The full public privacy and contact pages are still being prepared.

Lexivanto is an independent project, not affiliated with other word-game brands.
