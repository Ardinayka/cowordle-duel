A two-player word game you can play right now

Two players, one hidden five-letter word. Lexivanto runs in your phone or computer browser, free, with no download.

Play now

Three ways to play against each other

Duel
You both lock in a guess without seeing the other's. Both guesses reveal together on one shared board, so each of you learns from the other's clues. Solve it first to win; if you both solve in the same round, the earlier correct guess wins.
Race
Same word, separate boards. The first correct guess wins, however many guesses it took.
Word Swap
With a friend only: each of you picks the word the other must guess. Fewer guesses wins.

Prefer to play on the same side? Try Co-op.

Who to play

  • A friend. On Play, choose Play a friend, pick a game, then send an invitation link or invite someone from your friends list.
  • Someone online, just for fun. Choose Normal to find a real opponent for a Duel. It is never rated, and guests can play.
  • Someone online, for rating. Choose Competitive for rated best-of-3 Duels against players near your rating, with tiers from Bronze to Master. Sign in with Google and play 5 Normal games first.
  • No one around? Practice against a bot.

Questions

Is it free?
Yes. There are no ads and nothing to buy.
Do both players need an account?
No. Guests can play friends by link and play Normal games. Competitive games and a saved friends list need a Google sign-in.
Can we play on two different phones?
Yes. Each player uses their own phone or computer; send the invitation link by any messaging app.
Is there a time limit?
Competitive Duels are best of 3 words with 45 seconds per guess. Normal Duels give the second player 30 seconds to reply once the first has locked a guess. Games with a friend have no clock unless the host adds one. Race never has a clock.
