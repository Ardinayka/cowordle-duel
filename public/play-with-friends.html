Play a word game with friends

Create a private Lexivanto room and invite one friend.

  1. Choose Duel, Race or Co-op.
  2. Enter your nickname if playing as a guest, then choose A friend.
  3. Review or change the rules and select Create invitation link.
  4. Send the link privately. Your friend can also enter the 12-character code under Join room.
  5. Your friend reviews the rules and selects I'm ready. The host is already ready.

Your room, your rules

Private Duel and Co-op offer no timer or 15, 30, 60 or 120 seconds. Private Race offers no timer or one, five or ten minutes.

Keep the invitation private

Rooms hold two players and expire after 24 hours. Anyone with the invitation can take an available seat. Your seat is kept in this browser tab; closing the tab can lose access, even if you signed in with Google.

Leaving a competitive match forfeits it. Leaving Co-op ends the team game. After a match, choose Play again, review the board or return to Game modes.
