Play word games with friends

Send a link, or invite someone from your friends list.

Start a game with a friend

  1. On Play, choose Play a friend.
  2. Pick a game: Duel, Race, Co-op or Word Swap.
  3. Choose Create invitation link and send it, or Find or invite a friend to invite someone from your friends list.
  4. Your friend opens the invitation and confirms they are ready.

A guest can join through a link without a Google account. Share it privately: anyone with the link can take the free seat. The room lasts up to 24 hours.

Add a friend

  1. Sign in with Google, open Me and choose a unique username.
  2. Open Friends and search for your friend's exact username or player tag.
  3. Choose Add friend. They must accept the request.

You can also open an opponent's avatar during a game and send a friend request from there.

Invitations and the inbox

When a friend invites you, a banner appears on whatever screen you are on, with a countdown, a short chime and vibration if you turned those on. An invitation lasts five minutes. If you miss it, it stays in Inbox for seven days with Challenge back. In the lobby, the sender sees whether the invitation was seen, accepted or declined.

Change the next game

Friend games have no clock by default. After a game, the host can choose Change settings to pick another game or add a timer in the same room. Both players confirm they are ready again.

Word Swap

Each player types a word for the other to guess and presses Enter to lock it. Neither sees the other's word. Once both are locked, you guess on separate boards; fewer guesses wins.

Stay in control

Open a player's avatar for friend and safety actions. Blocking removes the friendship and stops requests, invitations and future matchmaking with that account. It does not end the game in progress. Manage blocked players in Friends.

Reconnecting or Offline describes the other player's connection. It does not add a timer to an untimed game. Use Back to leave.
