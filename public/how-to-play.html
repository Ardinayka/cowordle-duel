How to play Lexivanto

Learn the clues and start your first five-letter word game.

Make your first guess

Enter a valid five-letter word using the on-screen keyboard or your own keyboard, then press Enter. Use the clues to improve your next guess.

Cyan ● — right place
The letter is in the correct position.
Amber ↔ — wrong place
The letter is in the word, but somewhere else.
Slate × — not present
The letter is absent, or there is no additional copy of it.

Choose how to play

Choose Duel, Race or Co-op, then Online, A friend or A bot. Guest play needs a nickname; Google sign-in is optional. Easy, Medium and Hard bots let you practise without a timer.

Words and languages

English accepts a broad UK and US word list, including plurals and inflections. Secret answers come from a separate curated list. Portuguese is a preview: enter accented letters and Ç as plain letters.
