How to play Lexivanto

Find the hidden five-letter English word. Use each clue to make your next guess count.

  1. On Play, choose Competitive (rated, sign-in needed), Normal (online, not rated), Play a friend or Practise vs bot.
  2. For a friend or a bot, pick the game: Duel, Race, Co-op or Word Swap (friends only).
  3. Guests get a name automatically; signed-in players use their username.
  4. Type five letters with the game keyboard or your physical keyboard. Press Enter to submit.

Read the clues

Square with a tick · correct position
This letter is in the correct place.
Circle · another position
The word contains this letter somewhere else.
Flat and dim · absent
This letter is absent, or the answer contains no additional copy of it.

The shapes appear on the keyboard too, so the clues never depend on colour alone. Repeated letters receive only as many positive clues as the answer contains.

Choose your pace

All bots are untimed, with Easy, Medium and Hard levels. Friendly Duel and Co-op games have no time limit by default; the host can add one. Race is always untimed and the first correct guess wins.

In online Duel, the first locked word starts the other player's 30-second reply clock. Both guesses reveal after the reply. Online Co-op gives each player 30 seconds for their turn.

Which words count?

English accepts a broad UK and US word list, including many plurals and inflections. Secret answers are curated separately. Use five letters without punctuation. A revealed word cannot be repeated on the same board.

Read the mode rules or invite a friend. Word-list and font credits remain available in the game's help.
